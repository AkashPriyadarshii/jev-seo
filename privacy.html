Privacy policy

This website collects nothing. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no accounts, no forms. Pages are static files served over HTTPS. The jev-seo CLI tool runs entirely on your machine: audits read your local files, rank history stays in a local SQLite database, and only the semantic scoring calls reach the TypeSafe API with the page content you explicitly pass. Nothing is sold, shared, or retained by this site because nothing is collected. Questions: https://github.com/AkashPriyadarshii/jev-seo/issues.
